PureAudiobooks is a 100% FOSS, ad-free, tracker-free audiobook player built purely on AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Room, Media3, and WorkManager with zero third-party dependencies.

What makes PureAudiobooks different from Voice or AudioAnchor:
- Live Stereo / Mono-Fix (L+R, Smart, L, R): Repair broken single-channel audiobook rips or phase cancellation per chapter, per book, or globally, without forcing system-wide Mono audio.
- Smart Album-Tag Conflict Resolver & Splitter: Detects missing or mixed ALBUM tags in a folder and lets you choose folder names, tags, custom metadata, or split a multi-book folder into separate audiobooks by Album tag.
- Side-by-Side Online Metadata Diff: Optionally query iTunes and Open Library on demand, compare candidates field-by-field before applying, and save portable info.txt and cover.jpg sidecar files locally.
- 2-Stage Trash & Auto-Move Finished Books: Soft-delete books to an in-app trash with configurable retention (Immediate, 24h, 48h, 72h) and optional auto-move rules for finished books.
- Symmetric 5-Setting Audio Overrides: Configure playback speed (0.50x to 3.00x in 0.02x steps with live speed-adjusted remaining time), silence trimming, normalization, Mono mode, and smart rewind globally or per book, with 1-tap Apply to all.
- SAF Folder Scanning, Bookmarks & Full JSON Backup: Supports flat, author, and multi-disc folders (MP3, M4B, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, WAV), UI/text zoom (80% to 160%), and English/German UI.
