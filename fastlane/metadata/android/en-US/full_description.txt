PureAudiobooks is a straightforward, privacy-first audiobook player and library manager for Android. Point it at your audiobook folder, track remaining listening time at a glance, and listen offline without accounts, ads, or tracking.

Features:
- SAF Folder Scanning: Pick any folder on internal storage or SD card via Android Storage Access Framework. Supports flat folders, author subfolders, multi-disc sets, and MP3, M4B, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, and WAV.
- Clear Listening States: Separates Continue listening (with speed-adjusted remaining time, progress bar, and 1-tap Play/Pause) from Not started and Finished books.
- Audio DSP & Unified Player: Fine playback speed (0.50x to 3.00x in 0.02x steps), silence trimming (Off, Soft, Medium, Strong), volume normalization, live Stereo / Mono-Fix (L+R, Smart, L, R), and sleep timer (5 to 120 min or end of chapter).
- Global & Per-Book Overrides: Set global audio defaults and override speed, silence trimming, normalization, Mono mode, or smart rewind per audiobook.
- Metadata & Tag Resolution: Natural track sorting, tag conflict detection, album splitting, bookmarks with notes, and optional on-demand iTunes and Open Library metadata lookup saved locally as info.txt and cover.jpg.
- Themes, Trash & Backup: System, Light, and Dark themes with 6 accent colors, UI and text zoom (80% to 160%), soft-delete trash with configurable retention, and full JSON backup and restore.
